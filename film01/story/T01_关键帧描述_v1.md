## 关键帧（每段 Seedance 一对：首帧和尾帧）

> 方向规则：书桌靠右墙，她面朝画面右侧。饭碗在她左手边，也就是靠近镜头的前景。她左手拿勺子，尾巴从椅子左侧垂下来。

### S01 门缝（推近）
**S01_first**
View from a dark hallway through a door that is open only a crack. The left and right thirds of the image are the dark wooden door and door frame in near-black shadow. Through the narrow vertical gap of warm light in the center we see into the study: the tiny chibi girl sits on the big black office swivel chair, seen from behind at a three-quarter angle from her left, facing a glowing monitor on a desk against the right wall. On the near end of the desk, at her left side, sits the gigantic white rice bowl, almost as wide as she is tall, piled with a mountain of rice. Her whale tail hangs over the left side of the chair and her feet dangle above the floor.

**S01_last**
The same view, with the camera moved much closer to the door gap: the gap now fills the middle half of the image, and the door edges are blurry dark shapes at the left and right edges. She and the gigantic rice bowl appear larger. Everything else is unchanged.

### S02 偷吃
**S02_first**
Seen through the door gap: a thin, blurred, near-black strip of the door edge runs down the far left of the image. Medium shot of the girl from her left side; she faces screen right toward the glowing monitor. She sits on the oversized chair, feet dangling, tail hanging over the left side of the seat. Her right hand rests on the keyboard pretending to type. Her left hand lifts a big spoon heaped with white rice toward her wide-open mouth, eyes sparkling with excitement. The gigantic rice bowl sits on the desk in the foreground between her and us.

**S02_last**
The same framing. The spoon in her left hand is now empty; her cheeks are puffed out round like a hamster stuffed with rice, a few grains of rice are stuck on her cheek and at the corner of her mouth, her eyes are happily closed in a ^ ^ shape, and she has a little blush. Her tail is lifted and curled happily.

### S04 被发现
**S04_first**
The same room seen from the doorway at standing eye level. The door is half open: its edge is visible on the far left of the image, swinging inward. Warm light from the hallway behind the viewer spills in as a narrow wedge across the floor. The girl is still at the desk, seen from her left-back three-quarter angle, unaware, chewing with puffed cheeks, the spoon sticking out of her mouth, her left hand resting on the gigantic rice bowl.

**S04_last**
The same view with the door now fully open (out of frame). A bright wedge of warm hallway light covers the floor and the girl, and the long dark shadow of a person (only the shadow; no person visible) falls across the floor and over her. She has turned her head back over her left shoulder toward the viewer and is frozen: eyes wide open in shock with tiny pupils, the spoon still sticking out of her puffed mouth, a drop of sweat on her temple, her fin ears raised stiffly, her tail stiff and straight.

### S05 打翻摔倒
**S05_first**
The same doorway view, slightly wider. The girl is in full comic panic: she has swung around on the chair to face the viewer, both arms flailing up in the air, the spoon flying out of her mouth, comedic tears at the corners of her eyes, mouth open in a scream. Her left elbow is just hitting the rim of the gigantic rice bowl on the desk. The warm light and the long shadow from the doorway still fall over her.

**S05_last**
The same view. The office chair is tipping over backward at a steep angle, with the girl falling backward off it, arms, legs and tail flailing in the air. Above her, the gigantic rice bowl has flipped upside down in mid-air, and a big cloud of white rice grains scatters through the air.

### S06 被盖住
**S06_first**
Low camera angle at floor level. The gigantic white rice bowl lies upside down on the wooden floor like a dome, completely covering the girl; she cannot be seen at all. Only her dark-navy whale tail with its pale underside and fluke sticks out from under the rim of the bowl toward the viewer, straight and stiff. Rice grains are scattered all over the floor. Behind the bowl, the black office chair lies tipped over on its back, one wheel still spinning. Warm light from the doorway lies across the floor, and the cold blue glow of the monitor is in the background.

**S06_last**
The same framing. The whale tail now lies limp and flat on the floor, and a single grain of rice rests on top of the tail fluke. Everything else is unchanged.

## 统一构图规则（附在每张关键帧提示词的末尾）
Wide landscape image; keep all important content inside the central horizontal band and leave plain margin at the very top and bottom, because the image will be cropped to 16:9. Keep the room layout consistent with the reference scene sheet: the desk is against the right wall and the girl faces screen right. No motion blur. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark anywhere; the monitor screen shows only blurry abstract colored shapes.

## 首尾帧自查清单（导演逐张放大检查用）
- [ ] 角色特征齐全：呆毛、女仆头饰、左侧蓝色蝴蝶结、横向的鳍耳朵、深蓝到天蓝的渐变发色、围裙上的鲸鱼、领口黑色蝴蝶结、深蓝色尾巴
- [ ] 方向正确：书桌在右墙，她面朝画面右侧；饭碗在前景，在她左手边；勺子在左手
- [ ] 饭碗大小：直径和她的身高差不多，前后各帧一致
- [ ] 手：手指数量对，没有多出来的手
- [ ] 首帧和尾帧：同一机位（S01 除外，它本身就是推镜头），光线一致，尺寸一致
- [ ] 画面上没有任何文字，屏幕只有模糊色块
- [ ] 16:9 裁切后，重要内容没有被裁掉
